## 2. Visual Identity Summary

Yun Seo-ha is a Korean high school student with a youthful, understated appearance. She has a soft oval face, a low-volume straight bob, thin see-through bangs, and a natural slim build. Her visual identity should feel ordinary, restrained, and realistic rather than glamorous or stylized.

### Primary Identity Anchors

1. The same youthful Korean facial identity
2. A straight bob ending at mid-neck, clearly above the shoulders
3. Thin see-through bangs
4. Three small silver hairpins at her left temple
5. A white school shirt with loosely rolled sleeves and a dark navy tie
6. A thin black band on her left wrist

## 3. Overall Appearance

- Youthful face appropriate for a high school student
- Average apparent height
- Natural slim build
- Narrow, relaxed shoulders
- Realistic teenage body proportions
- Balanced silhouette when viewed from the front
- Neat but unremarkable first impression
- No exaggerated curves, visible muscular definition, or fashion-model proportions
- No glamorous, seductive, or highly stylized presentation

## 4. Facial Features

- **Face shape:** Soft oval
- **Chin:** Small and rounded, never sharply pointed
- **Eyes:** Dark brown, almond-shaped
- **Eye angle:** Nearly horizontal with a very slight upward tilt at the outer corners
- **Eyebrows:** Thin, dark, and relatively straight
- **Nose:** Small and straight
- **Lips:** Small, naturally proportioned, muted pink
- **Skin tone:** Light neutral tone; neither unnaturally pale nor excessively glossy
- **Default expression:** Almost neutral, with a faint sense of alertness and fatigue
- **Makeup:** Minimal and natural

### Facial Restrictions

- No heavy eye makeup
- No thick eyeliner
- No exaggerated eyelashes
- No highly saturated lipstick
- No sharply sculpted jawline
- No adult fashion-model appearance
- Do not alter the eye shape, facial proportions, or apparent age between shots

## 5. Hairstyle

- **Color:** Natural near-black or extremely dark brown
- **Style:** Low-volume straight bob
- **Length:** Approximately 5–7 cm below the jawline
- **Landmark:** Hair ends around the middle of the neck and does not touch the shoulders
- **Layering:** None or extremely subtle
- **Ends:** Nearly straight, with little to no inward C-curl
- **Part:** Natural near-center part
- **Bangs (DEC-005 갱신):** Dense blunt full bangs, near-eyebrow length — see-through/thin bangs이던 이전 서술을 대체
- **Bang length:** At or just above the eyebrows
- **Ear visibility:** Partially covered by hair on both sides in the new reference; exact left/right asymmetry is not yet re-confirmed (미결 — DEC-005 참고)
- **Signature detail (DEC-005 갱신):** One decorative pearl-bead hairpin/barrette, worn near the temple. 정확한 좌/우 위치는 아직 인간이 명시적으로 확인하지 않았으므로 새 primary reference 이미지를 우선한다.

### Hair Restrictions

- No chin-length short bob
- No shoulder-length or collarbone-length hair
- No curls, waves, or perm texture
- No strong inward C-curl
- No high crown volume
- No fully exposed forehead
- No dyed or highlighted hair
- 머리핀 개수·위치를 primary reference 이미지와 다르게 임의로 바꾸지 않는다 (정확한 좌/우 규칙은 미결 — DEC-005 참고)

## 6. Body Proportions and Posture

- Natural proportions of approximately 7–7.5 heads tall
- Average apparent height; exact height is not yet fixed
- Slim arms and legs without making them unnaturally long or thin
- Narrow, naturally relaxed shoulders
- Realistic head-to-body ratio
- Neutral standing posture facing forward when no scene-specific pose is required
- Arms rest naturally at her sides in the base reference pose
- Weight is distributed evenly across both feet

### Proportion and Posture Restrictions

- No oversized or undersized head
- No elongated fashion-illustration proportions
- No extreme hourglass silhouette
- No muscular or athletic exaggeration
- No forced chest-forward posture
- No runway pose, hip tilt, or glamorous fashion stance

## 7. Canonical School Uniform

### Shirt

- Plain white long-sleeved school shirt
- Neat, natural fit that is neither skin-tight nor oversized
- Both sleeves loosely rolled one or two times
- Rolled cuffs sit below the elbows
- Sleeve rolls must look slightly rough and asymmetrical rather than perfectly folded

### Tie

- Solid dark navy necktie
- Slightly relaxed at the collar but still properly tied
- Never fully loosened or hanging open
- Tip ends around the waistline

### Skirt

- Very dark navy pleated school skirt, close to black
- Consistent pleat structure and overall length
- Natural movement and fabric folds are allowed during action

### Socks

- White crew socks
- Soft, natural folds around the ankles
- Consistent height across shots

### Shoes

- White low-top retro sneakers
- Black side stripes
- Gum rubber soles
- Same silhouette, color blocking, and sole shape in every shot

## 10. Camera and Lighting Reference

For neutral reference generation:

- Soft, even, neutral studio lighting
- Plain neutral-gray background
- Eye-level camera
- Approximately 50 mm full-frame-equivalent lens perspective
- No fisheye or extreme wide-angle distortion
- Even exposure that preserves facial structure and uniform colors
- Full-body framing should include the entire hairstyle and both shoes
- Keep the camera square to the character for front, side, and rear turnaround views

For cinematic scenes, lighting and color grading may change, but the face must remain identifiable and the skin tone must not shift enough to suggest a different person or ethnicity.
