# Classroom Reference

## Purpose
---
This document defines the fixed visual and spatial rules for generating consistent reference images of the same Korean school classroom from multiple camera angles.

The classroom must remain visually coherent across all future generations. Camera position may change, but the architecture, furniture design, fixed fixtures, wall layout, window placement, door style, locker structure, and major classroom objects must remain consistent unless explicitly revised.

## Primary Reference Hierarchy
---
Use the references in the following priority order:

1. **Final rear-facing classroom reference**
   - Viewpoint: from the teacher's podium, looking directly toward the rear wall
   - Defines the rear-wall geometry, locker layout, rear-side windows and doors, desk orientation, and overall room depth

2. **Final front-facing classroom reference**
   - Viewpoint: from the center rear of the classroom, looking directly toward the blackboard
   - Defines the front-wall geometry, blackboard, teacher's podium, TV placement, front door, front-side windows, desk alignment, and classroom symmetry

3. **Final side/diagonal classroom reference**
   - Defines the established material palette, lighting, locker appearance, desk-and-chair design, window style, wall finish, and round wall clock

4. **Corridor and classroom-door reference**
   - Defines the wooden classroom door style, transom glazing, warm wood tone, frame proportions, and adjacent corridor-facing glazing

5. **Original desk-and-chair object reference**
   - Defines the intended one-person desk and matching chair design

## Overall Visual Style
---
Create a **photorealistic Korean school classroom**.

The classroom should feel:
- realistic
- clean but slightly aged
- functional rather than luxurious
- naturally lit
- visually consistent with a typical Korean middle-school or high-school classroom

Use:
- off-white upper walls
- muted pale green or gray-green lower wall sections
- warm wood window and door frames
- light neutral or speckled gray flooring
- white acoustic-tile ceiling
- ordinary fluorescent ceiling fixtures
- bright natural daylight from the window side

Avoid:
- modern luxury-school interiors
- excessive decoration
- dramatic cinematic lighting
- stylized or illustration-like rendering
- unnecessary furniture or wall ornaments

## Classroom Dimensions and Composition
---
The classroom should preserve the same overall scale and proportions established in the primary references.

The room must maintain:
- realistic Korean classroom width and depth
- a clear central axis from the rear wall to the blackboard
- consistent aisle widths
- consistent desk spacing
- the same approximate ceiling height
- the same window-wall proportions
- the same front-wall and rear-wall alignment

The classroom front and rear should read as parts of the same rectangular room.

## Desk Layout
---
The student desks must remain arranged in a regular grid.

Use:
- **5 columns**
- **5 desks in each column**
- approximately **25 student desks total**

The five-column structure must remain clearly readable from centered front-facing and rear-facing views.

The desks must:
- remain evenly spaced
- maintain practical walking aisles
- align consistently toward the blackboard
- not form irregular diagonal clusters
- not increase or decrease in count unless explicitly requested

## Desks and Chairs
---
Use the established desk-and-chair design consistently throughout the classroom.

Each student station consists of:
- one individual desk
- one separate matching chair

### Desk
- light gray or pale beige desktop
- dark edge trim
- gray metal frame
- green front storage/modesty panel beneath the desktop

### Chair
- muted olive-green molded-plastic seat
- muted olive-green molded-plastic backrest
- gray metal frame

All desks and chairs must belong to the same model family.

Do not:
- mix different chair colors
- introduce wooden student chairs
- use paired two-person desks
- redesign the desk frame between camera angles

## Front Wall
---
The front wall is defined by the final front-facing classroom reference.

### Blackboard
- a large dark green **blackboard** must be centered on the front wall
- the main writing surface should occupy the central portion of the wall
- lighter green side panels may flank the central board
- the board should remain horizontally aligned and realistic in proportion

### A4 Notices
A4-size printed notices must be attached on **both sides of the blackboard**.

The notices should:
- appear as ordinary school notices, schedules, charts, or printed sheets
- remain secondary visual elements
- not cover the central writing area
- stay concentrated on the side-board areas

### Teacher's Podium
A wooden teacher's podium must be positioned on the classroom centerline in front of the blackboard.

It should:
- face the student desks
- remain centered
- use a simple light-to-medium wood finish
- retain approximately the same dimensions across future views

Small ordinary teacher supplies may appear on top, but avoid clutter.

### TV
A black flat-panel TV must be mounted in the **upper-left area of the classroom front wall**.

The TV should:
- remain powered off
- be mounted high enough to be visible over the desks
- remain left of the main blackboard
- preserve the same approximate size and mounting position between views

## Front Door
---
The visible front classroom door must follow the corridor reference as closely as possible.

Use:
- warm medium-brown wood
- vertically proportioned double-leaf classroom doors
- glazed upper sections
- solid wooden lower sections
- narrow vertical glass panes
- matching wooden frames
- transom glazing above the door where visible

The interior-facing view of the door must look like the inside surface of the same corridor door system.

### Class Placard Rule
**No classroom number placard or class sign may be visible from inside the classroom.**

This applies to:
- the front-facing classroom view
- the rear-facing classroom view
- side views from inside the classroom
- any future interior camera angle

A class placard such as **"2-7"** may exist only on the **corridor-facing exterior side** of the doorway and must not appear inside the room.

## Rear Wall
---
The rear wall must remain visually simple and is defined by the final rear-facing classroom reference.

### Lockers
A long row of light-wood student lockers must run horizontally along the rear wall.

The lockers must be:
- arranged in **exactly two stacked tiers**
- uniform in width
- uniform in depth
- visually aligned

The **upper and lower locker tiers must have equal vertical height**.

Do not:
- make the upper tier shorter
- make the lower tier taller
- use three tiers
- insert open shelving between lockers
- vary locker door proportions arbitrarily

Each locker door should have:
- a small label area
- a dark handle or lock fixture

### Rear Wall Surface
The rear wall above the lockers must remain mostly plain.

Do not include:
- bulletin boards
- large notice boards
- decorative display boards
- chalkboards
- clusters of posters or paper notices

## Window System
---
The classroom window system must remain consistent across all camera angles.

Use:
- large rectangular windows
- warm wood-toned frames
- repeated multi-pane divisions
- light curtains where established
- bright daylight
- realistic exterior greenery or school-building context

The windows should remain on the same side of the classroom across all reference images.

## Front-Rear Symmetry
---
When generating opposite-facing views, preserve the classroom's architectural logic.

The front-facing and rear-facing views should be treated as opposite views inside the same rectangular classroom.

Therefore:
- doors must remain on the same wall side relative to the room
- windows must remain on the same wall side relative to the room
- the front and rear views must not mirror the classroom incorrectly
- fixed architectural elements must maintain consistent left/right relationships when the camera turns 180 degrees

Use the final front-facing reference to infer the corresponding rear-facing door and window placement, and use the final rear-facing reference to verify the same room geometry.

## Round Wall Clock
---
A simple round wall clock must remain mounted on the **window-side column/wall area**.

The clock should:
- be circular
- use a simple white or light face
- have dark numerals and hands
- look like an ordinary Korean classroom wall clock
- remain approximately in the same architectural location

The clock replaces the wall-mounted fan that previously occupied that location.

Do not reintroduce a fan at that exact position.

## Ceiling
---
The ceiling must remain flat and visually continuous.

Include:
- white acoustic ceiling tiles
- fluorescent light fixtures
- ceiling-mounted air-conditioning cassette where established
- ordinary ceiling fans where already established and physically plausible

Do **not** include:
- a large structural beam crossing the middle of the classroom ceiling
- exposed blue structural beams
- heavy industrial ceiling members
- arbitrary ceiling redesigns between views

## Removed Elements
---
The following elements must not reappear:

- the wall text **"지금도 충분히 좋아"**
- similar motivational posters on the window-side column
- the rear-wall bulletin board
- large rear-wall notice boards
- the central ceiling beam
- an interior-visible classroom placard such as **"2-7"**
- a wall-mounted fan in the round-clock position

## Camera Reference Views
---
### Front-Facing Reference
Camera position:
- centered near the rear wall
- eye level
- looking directly toward the blackboard
- centered on the teacher's podium and blackboard

Must clearly show:
- 5-column × 5-row desk grid
- central blackboard
- teacher's podium
- upper-left TV
- A4 notices on both sides of the blackboard
- front classroom door
- window side
- no visible class placard

### Rear-Facing Reference
Camera position:
- centered at or immediately behind the teacher's podium
- eye level
- looking directly toward the rear wall

Must clearly show:
- the same 5-column × 5-row desk grid from the opposite direction
- two-tier equal-height lockers
- rear wall
- window side
- classroom doors/windows consistent with the front-facing reference
- no visible class placard

### Side/Diagonal Reference
Use for:
- materials
- lighting
- window details
- desk proportions
- locker proportions
- wall clock location
- general classroom atmosphere

## Hard Consistency Constraints
---
The following rules are mandatory for future generations:

- photorealistic Korean classroom
- same room identity across all images
- 5 desk columns
- 5 desks per column
- approximately 25 desks total
- same olive-green desk-and-chair model family
- centered front blackboard
- centered teacher's podium
- TV mounted upper-left of front wall
- A4 notices on both sides of blackboard
- warm wooden classroom doors matching corridor reference
- no class placard visible from inside
- rear lockers in exactly 2 tiers
- upper and lower locker tiers equal in height
- rear wall without bulletin board
- round wall clock on window-side column
- no fan in the clock position
- no central ceiling beam
- same window-wall orientation
- consistent front/rear architectural symmetry
- no unnecessary decorative additions

## Generation Priority
---
When multiple references conflict, use this priority:

1. **Latest explicitly approved classroom image**
2. **Latest user correction**
3. **This reference document**
4. **Earlier classroom reference images**
5. **General realism**

Never overwrite an explicitly fixed detail merely to make the image look more typical or aesthetically balanced.

## Reuse Rule
---
For any future image of this classroom:

- preserve all fixed architecture
- preserve door and window positions
- preserve desk count and grid
- preserve desk-and-chair design
- preserve locker structure
- preserve front-wall layout
- preserve TV placement
- preserve podium placement
- preserve round wall clock
- preserve material palette
- preserve lighting character

Only change:
- camera position
- camera direction
- focal length within realistic limits
- explicitly requested temporary props
- explicitly requested story or character elements
